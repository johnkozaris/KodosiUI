# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Linux desktop

## Purpose

Kodosi is a terminal workbench for developers and coding agents. It keeps local
terminals close, makes them available from approved devices, and lets people
share selected terminals with friends they trust.

Missions provide a simple way to group people and terminals. Saved coding
conversations can be found and continued without Kodosi taking ownership of
provider data.

## Principles

- Keep the terminal primary and the surrounding interface quiet.
- Make common actions immediate; reveal secondary choices only when needed.
- Use plain language and native desktop behavior.
- Make access and control clear.
- Preserve files, provider history, identity, and user consent.
- Keep Mission membership separate from terminal access.

## Scope

The product is terminals, trusted people, Missions, saved conversation history,
and the few settings needed to support them. It is not an agent dashboard, task
board, or provider management system.

## Experience

Kodosi should feel like a focused workbench, not an administration console.
Keyboard access, screen-reader support, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and
reduced motion are part of the experience.
